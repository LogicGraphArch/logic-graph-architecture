# RFC 001: Architecture Overview & Diagnostic Scope

* **Status:** Draft
* **Author:** LogicGraphArch
* **Target Domain:** Swiss Legal Consistency & Structural Verification

## 1. Problem Statement
Legal frameworks and legislative drafts often contain structural dependencies, systemic circular references, or implicit logic conflicts. Traditional review relies heavily on narrative interpretation rather than formal dependency analysis.

## 2. Core Concepts
* **Legal Logic Nodes:** Representation of legal norms, conditions, and consequences as graph elements.
* **Structural Verification:** Identifying deadlocks, ambiguous precedence, and logical contradictions.
* **Scope Boundary:** Focus solely on structural logic and internal consistency, completely independent of political or normative positions.

## 3. Proposed Methodology
1. **Parsing & Tokenization:** Deconstructing legal articles into conditional logical rules.
2. **Graph Construction:** Building directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) of legal dependencies.
3. **Consistency Checking:** Running structural checks against core constitutional and statutory baselines.

## 4. Next Steps & Feedback Requested
* Architectural suggestions for standardizing legal node representations.
* Input on Swiss legal taxonomy integration.
