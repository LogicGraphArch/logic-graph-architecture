# RFC-001: Architecture Overview & Diagnostic Scope

- **Status:** Draft
- **Author:** LogicGraphArch
- **Target Domain:** Swiss Legal Consistency & Structural Verification

> This RFC is part of the Logic-Graph-Architecture project.
> See [README](README.md) for full project context and motivation.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Legal frameworks and legislative drafts often contain structural dependencies, systemic circular references, or implicit logic conflicts. Traditional review relies heavily on narrative interpretation rather than formal dependency analysis.

With documents like the Bilateral Agreements III (~23,000 pages), manual penetration at normal working pace is impossible – leading inevitably to opacity. A systematic, reproducible, machine-assisted structural analysis is needed.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **Legal Logic Nodes:** Representation of legal norms, conditions, and consequences as graph elements.
- **Structural Verification:** Identifying deadlocks, ambiguous precedence, and logical contradictions.
- **Scope Boundary:** Focus solely on structural logic and internal consistency, completely independent of political or normative positions.
- **Semantic Calibration:** Undefined legal terms are measured against historical court decisions to establish a statistical baseline – no judgment, only deviation measurement.

---

## 3. Proposed Methodology

1. **Parsing & Tokenization:** Deconstructing legal articles into conditional logical rules.
2. **Graph Construction:** Building directed graphs of legal dependencies with cycle detection – legal systems often contain mutual references that pure DAGs cannot represent.
3. **Consistency Checking:** Running structural checks against core constitutional and statutory baselines (Bundesverfassung, ZGB, OR, StGB).
4. **Semantic Calibration Layer:** Undefined legal terms (e.g. "reasonable", "proportionate", "adequate") are calibrated against historical Federal Court decisions (BGE). The system calculates a statistical corridor of established interpretation and flags deviations in new texts. No judgment – only deviation measurement.

> **Example:** If the filter encounters the term "reasonable" in a new legal text, it reads all relevant BGE decisions for that term in that context, calculates the statistical corridor, and reports: "In 92% of prior precedents, this term in context X implied a value of Y. The new text deviates from this expected value – or leaves it undefined." Where no sufficient BGE baseline exists, the system explicitly flags an "uncalibrated zone" rather than fabricating false precision.

5. **Consequence Mapping:** Visualising the consequence space of JA vs. NEIN decisions – which chain reactions, systemic interactions and long-term effects follow from each path?

---

## 4. Language Protocol

The system never uses normative legal judgments. Language is strictly that of structural analysis:

**Always use:** "logical tension field" – "system inconsistency" – "architectural stress point" – "structural deviation" – "deviation from historical corridor"

**Never use:** "unconstitutional" – "illegal" – "wrong" – "dangerous"

This language protocol is binding for the system output and all project communications.

## 5. Reproducibility & Trust

- Full open-source codebase under GNU GPL
- Cryptographic signatures on all outputs
- Every calculation locally verifiable by any citizen
- No black box – **Don't Trust, Verify**

---

## 6. Next Steps & Open Questions

Architectural suggestions and critical feedback explicitly welcome. Key open questions:

- **Graph model:** How should circular legal references (mutual dependencies between statutes) be handled in the graph model? Pure DAGs may be insufficient – what is the right structure?
- **Data scale:** Which graph database or knowledge graph architecture best fits BGE-scale historical data (~decades of Federal Court decisions)?
- **Semantic calibration:** How can semantic calibration be made deterministic and fully reproducible across different runs and environments?
- **Visualisation:** What 3D graph visualisation approaches best communicate structural tension fields to non-technical users (citizens) while remaining precise for experts?
- **Scope creep prevention:** How do we ensure the system remains strictly structural and never drifts into normative territory?
